The phrase is conceptual. It does not describe a sale or promise to buy, deliver or consume alcohol. A completed payment is intended as a voluntary personal gift, not payment under a contract for goods or services.

You must be at least 18, have legal capacity, be authorised to use the payment method, and meet PayPal's eligibility requirements. Do not continue if you do not understand the payment or cannot afford it.

4. Suggested amount and payment

The displayed pint figure is only a suggested gift amount. Check the amount and GBP currency on PayPal before approving it. The link requests a one-off payment; this site does not create a subscription or recurring charge.

PayPal processes the transaction under its own User Agreement and privacy statement. PayPal's eligibility, security, dispute and chargeback processes operate independently and are not changed by these terms.

Because a completed payment is intended as a gift, there is no contractual cancellation entitlement. If you email the address above with the PayPal transaction ID within 14 days, Stephan Pieri will normally consider a full refund as a gesture of goodwill. After 14 days, gifts are normally final. Suspected unauthorised or duplicate payments will still be reviewed promptly. Nothing here limits any right that cannot lawfully be excluded or your ability to use PayPal's dispute process.

Pint prices are dated estimates drawn from the sources shown on the site. They are not live venue prices, quotations or guarantees and may be incomplete, inaccurate or out of date. Please do not rely on them for financial, travel or purchasing decisions.

Privacy notice

Who is responsible

Stephan Pieri is responsible for the limited personal information described below. Contact stephan.pieri@gmail.com with a privacy question or request.

Manual and saved location choices

Choosing a city or region manually does not reveal your physical location. The selected pricing-zone ID and selection method are stored in your browser's session storage so the choice survives a reload in the same tab. This is used only to provide the feature you requested. Choose “Forget this location”, close the tab, or clear site data to remove it.

Using your location

Nothing requests location on page load. If you choose “Use my location”, your browser asks for permission and, if granted, sends your current coordinates directly to BigDataCloud to return a city or region. If coordinates are unavailable, BigDataCloud may estimate an area using the IP address sent with the request. Manual selection remains available instead.

This processing is based on your active request and, for browser coordinates, your consent. Stephan Pieri does not receive or keep the coordinates, IP address, postcode or full provider response. You can withdraw consent for future requests through browser permissions and use manual selection, although a request already sent cannot be recalled.

BigDataCloud handles data under its privacy and cookie policy.

Hosting information

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like other web hosts, GitHub may process connection information such as IP address, device, browser and security logs when serving the page. Stephan Pieri does not receive those hosting logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Payments and messages

Clicking the payment link takes you to PayPal. This site never receives card, bank or PayPal login credentials. If you complete a gift, PayPal may provide Stephan Pieri with transaction details such as your name, email address, amount, date, transaction ID and message, depending on the transaction and your PayPal settings.

Those details and any email you send are used only to administer gifts, answer you, provide refunds, keep necessary records, and prevent fraud or misuse. The lawful bases are legitimate interests in administering the voluntary gift and complying with legal obligations where they apply.

Read PayPal's Privacy Statement.

Cookies, analytics and automated decisions

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics. The temporary session-storage item described above is necessary to remember your requested selection. There is no profiling or automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect.

Sharing, transfers and retention

Personal information is not sold. It is handled only through the providers identified above. Those providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, using the safeguards described in their own privacy notices.

Session storage lasts only for the browser-tab session. Payment records and related correspondence are kept within PayPal or email only as long as reasonably needed for refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, tax and legal claims—normally no longer than six years unless a longer period is legally required.

Your rights

Depending on the information and lawful basis, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Legal exceptions may apply. Email the address above to make a request.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. You do not need to contact Stephan Pieri first.

Age and updates
